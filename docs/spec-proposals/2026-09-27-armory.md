# Proposed spec section: The Armory: earned upgrades the child chooses (2026-09-27)

**Status:** Proposal. Nothing is locked until Samarth stamps it.
**Decision owner:** Samarth · **Drafted:** 2026-09-27
**Disposition:** Additive. It builds on the Companion + BuildGoal lock (Opportunity §9.3, 2026-09-21) and the Gamification locks. It reopens none of them.
**Prototype:** `prototype/armory.js` and the Armory screen (Badges → Armory). Dev controls are under Behind the glass → Controls.

## 1. Intent

Dogfood finding (2026-09-27): the warrior should *show* growth, and the child should get to shape it. The child earns **forge tokens** from mastery. They spend tokens on permanent upgrades for the warrior, or save them for rarer ones. Rarity means a higher price, never chance.

## 2. Rules

1. **Tokens come from mastery and qualifying practice days, never from raw volume or XP.**
   - +`TOKENS_PER_PIECE` (stub: 1) for each `BuildPieceUnlock`.
   - +`TOKENS_PER_WARRIOR` (stub: 2) for each completed BuildGoal.
   - +`TOKENS_PER_QPD` (stub: 1) for each `QualifyingPracticeDay`, at most one a day.
   - All three sources are already QualifyingEvent projections, so no new mint path is added.
   - Why the qualifying-day source exists: first-time mastery events are finite. In a 12-week simulation, mastery-only tokens stall at about 35 for a strong learner and about 18 for a struggling one. Qualifying days keep tokens coming, and they reward showing up on separate days rather than question count.
2. **XP is never spent.** XP stays a score that only goes up (Gamification lock 2, "Never take XP away"). The XP ledger stays credit-only.
3. **Tokens are derived, not stored.** available = earned (from the event log) − the sum of claim costs. There is no token counter column (see CLAUDE.md, "no parallel counters").
4. **Claims are append-only and permanent.** Nothing claimed can be lost, taken back or expire. The child can swap between owned items and the starter look at any time.
5. **Fixed, visible catalog.** Every item's look and cost is shown up front. No random drops, loot boxes or mystery items (Arch "no random loot tables").
6. **Rarity = cost:**
   - common: 2 tokens
   - rare: 6 tokens
   - legendary: 15 tokens, and only after at least one completed warrior (`LEGENDARY_NEEDS_WARRIORS`, stub: 1)
   - The whole 14-item catalog costs 100 tokens, sized to last about one school term (see §5).
7. **New sets over time.** Once there is final art, add a new set about every 4 weeks, so a child who owns everything still has something to save for. Earlier sets stay available; nothing is removed or time-limited.
8. **No pressure.** No limited-time items, countdowns, "only today" offers, streak-gated sales or nagging. Saving up shows as progress ("9 of 15 tokens saved"), not as missing out.
9. **Off the practice path.** The Armory is reached from Badges. Practice stays home (IA §9). The session only mentions tokens inside an earned-piece moment ("New piece: Cape · +1 forge token").
10. **No money, ever.** Tokens can't be bought and there are no paid skips (Opportunity §9, "No paid skips for build pieces"). This extends the existing lock to tokens and items.
11. **The look follows the warrior everywhere:** Badges, the run companion, level-up and power-up moments. Only pieces the child has earned are drawn in the chosen style. Unearned slots stay as outlines.
12. **The parent view never shows XP** (unchanged). Whether the parent view shows the warrior's look is left to Phase D.

## 3. What this supersedes

Nothing is reversed. It narrows one line:
- IA "Cut from v1 nav: shop". The Armory is not a shop: no money, no XP spending, no randomness, and only earned tokens. If the "no shop" cut is read as "no spending of any earned currency", this section is an explicit exception, and it needs Samarth's stamp.

## 4. Hosted build (for build step 3)

| Table | Columns | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `armory_claims` | id, child_id, item_id, item_version, cost, claimed_at, policy_version | Append-only. The UNIQUE (child_id, item_id) constraint makes a claim idempotent |
| `armory_wearing` | child_id, slot, item_id | Mutable, one row per slot. Presentation state only |
| `armory_items` | id, version, slot, rarity, cost, art_ref | A content table, like `templates` |

- **Server authority:** the server recomputes available tokens inside the claim transaction and rejects a claim that can't be afforded. The client gets `{available, earned, spent}` plus item states. It never computes eligibility itself.
- **Tests:**
  - tokens = derived formula
  - a claim fails when unaffordable, or when it's legendary without a completed warrior
  - a same-item re-claim is a no-op
  - XP total never changes on a claim
  - no path mints tokens from HonestAttempt alone

## 5. Open questions

- **Catalog size (decided 2026-09-27: both).** At the original prices (1/3/5, 40 in total), a strong learner could own nearly everything by week 3, because qualifying-day tokens add about 7 a week. Samarth chose to raise prices to 2/6/15 (100 in total) now, and to add new sets about every 4 weeks once there is final art (rule 7). The price stubs still need dogfood calibration.
- **Warrior art direction:** a full-body chibi knight (prototyped 2026-09-27). Gear is most of the silhouette, and higher tiers change shape, not just colour. A persistent gold aura is the set bonus for wearing all four legendaries.

- **Stub values:** tokens per piece or warrior, and the costs. They're uncalibrated; dogfood pacing decides them.
- **Naming:** "forge tokens" is a placeholder. Kid-test the name.
- **Catalog size and art:** the prototype has 14 items in placeholder SVG. Final art needs an illustrator, and should use shape changes rather than colour alone.
- **Completed warriors:** does a finished warrior go to a "hall" that keeps its look, or does the next warrior inherit the chosen gear? The prototype does the latter.
